The raw results behind Section VII of the paper, from one full run of
./run.sh full on the testbed (Google Cloud e2-standard-8 VM: 8 vCPU Intel Xeon
2.2 GHz, 31 GiB RAM, Ubuntu 22.04, Python 3.12.10, Go 1.24.7), started
30 Oct 2026. run_info.json lists every stage, its status and its duration.

REPORT.md / REPORT.html  summary generated by the pipeline
numbers.json             every number quoted in Section VII (bench/figures.py)
fig6_* ... fig10_*       the result figures (Figs. 7-11 of the manuscript)
n4fine/                  n = 4 ledger sweep (Fig. 8, Table XIX)
bundles/                 tag-bundling runs (Fig. 9)
*_n7_*, *_n10_*          ledger runs with 7 and 10 hospitals
fhir_workload*.csv       Synthea and MIMIC-IV FHIR workloads (Table XXII)
logs/                    the log of every stage

In the first run the Fabric stages failed on a peer configuration error
(logs/failed-run1, run_info.run1.json); after the fix in
fabric/network/pledge_net.py those stages were rerun and all stages are "ok".
Usernames in paths have been replaced by "user".
